HELP & GUIDANCE

Questions about school travel?

Explore the platform guidance or contact the programme support team for assistance with registration and trip submissions.

Who can use NSTMS?

Schools, parents or guardians, drivers and authorised government officers have role-specific access.

Does online approval replace a vehicle inspection?

No. The platform helps coordinate and record checks. Physical inspections and legal decisions must be completed by the relevant authorised officials.

Can parents follow trip updates?

The planned parent portal provides consent, trip information and status updates according to the permissions granted to each parent or guardian.
